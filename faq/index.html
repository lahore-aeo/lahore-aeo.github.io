AEO FAQ

Does AEO replace SEO?

No. AEO builds on crawlability, useful content, sound site structure, and other SEO foundations.

Can schema guarantee a Google or AI citation?

No. Structured data can clarify supported information, but search platforms decide what to show and may change their systems.

Should every article have an FAQ section?

No. Add questions only when they help readers and reflect real query needs. Avoid repetitive FAQ blocks added solely to target keywords.

How soon will AEO work?

There is no fixed timeline or guaranteed result. Indexing, competition, site quality, and platform behavior all affect outcomes. Track changes over time.
