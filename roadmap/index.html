A focused 90-day AEO roadmap

Weeks 1–2 · Audit

Choose one topic area. Check indexation, analytics, important URLs, existing answers, and competing user needs.

Weeks 3–4 · Plan

Group queries by intent. Pick one pillar page and a small number of genuinely distinct supporting guides.

Weeks 5–8 · Improve

Strengthen the pages, add evidence and contextual links, fix technical issues, and validate suitable schema.

Weeks 9–12 · Review

Measure search and business outcomes, sample AI answers, correct gaps, and prioritize the next cluster based on evidence.
